\begin{standardframe}{One deck, several PDFs}
	\small
	Students need the slides before a lecture, but without the answers to the
	questions you ask; after the course they should get everything. Instead of
	three decks, you keep one: each compile produces one \emph{deck version},
	and the slides say what each version shows.
	\par\smallskip
	\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
		\begin{column}{0.42\linewidth}
			\shrinklists{1}
			\begin{itemize}
				\item \textbf{instructor}: everything, step by step
				\item \textbf{during-course}: answers left out
				\item \textbf{after-course}: everything, all at once
				\item Also: a \textbf{presenter} PDF with your notes, and after-course \textbf{per chapter}
				\item The footer names the version
			\end{itemize}
		\end{column}
