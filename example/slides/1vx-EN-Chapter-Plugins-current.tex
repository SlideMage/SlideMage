\begin{sectionframe}{Plugins}
	\begin{minipage}[t]{0.9\linewidth}\small
		A plugin adds a command, a Deck menu entry, a settings tab or a
		side-panel box from outside SlideMage's core. SlideMage works without
		plugins; none is installed by default. The AI (Claude, Ollama) and
		CampusOnline are plugins: without them SlideMage is a complete editor.
	\end{minipage}\par\medskip
