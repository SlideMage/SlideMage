\begin{standardframe}{Getting slides out}
	\small
	On SlideMage's main screen, each row of the deck table has five checkboxes.
	Every one you tick makes a PDF of its own when you press \emph{Create PDF}.
	The first three are the deck versions; the other two are variants of them.
	\par\medskip
	{\footnotesize
	\begin{gothamtable}[gap=10pt]{ll}
		\toprule
		Checkbox & The PDF you get \\
		\midrule
		Instructor & the whole deck, every reveal step on a page of its own \\
		During-course & the student version for the course itself, answers left out \\
		After-course & the student version for afterwards: every slide in its final state \\
		After-course, per chapter & the after-course version, one PDF per chapter \\
		Presenter & each slide beside its speaker notes (\SMCmd{note}), for a presenter app \\
		\bottomrule
	\end{gothamtable}\par}
	\vspace{10pt}
	\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
		\begin{column}{0.66\linewidth}
			\shrinklists{2}
			\begin{itemize}
				\item The PDFs go to the \SMCode{pdf/} folder, or where the deck's \emph{Settings} say under \emph{Where the PDFs go}
				\item \emph{Open pdf} in the row opens the latest PDF
			\end{itemize}
		\end{column}

			{\scriptsize a presenter page\par}
		\end{column}
	\end{columns}
\end{standardframe}
