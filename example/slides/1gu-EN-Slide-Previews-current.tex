\setSlideVersion{1-0-0}
\begin{standardframe}{Slide previews}
	\small
	SlideMage keeps a small picture of every slide, taken from its last compile, so you recognize slides at a glance: as thumbnails in the slide list and in \emph{Preview}. Previews belong to a template: a slide compiled in two templates has two.
	\par\medskip
	\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
		\begin{column}{0.37\linewidth}
			{\setlength{\fboxsep}{0pt}\fcolorbox{c_primary_gray}{white}{\includegraphics[width=\dimexpr\linewidth-0.8pt\relax]{media/screenshot-slidemage-slide-preview.png}}}
		\end{column}
		\begin{column}{0.60\linewidth}
			\shrinklists{2}
			\begin{itemize}
				\item \emph{Preview} shows the saved picture at once; chips say what it is not showing: a pinned version, another language, a missing translation, a view that skips the slide
				\item A list row says \emph{preview old} when the source is newer; \emph{Render} makes a fresh one in the chosen view and language
				\item Every compile that includes the slide refreshes it: a cache, not a live view
				\item Three-dot menu $\rightarrow$ \emph{Housekeeping} $\rightarrow$ \emph{Update slide previews} compiles every slide once
			\end{itemize}
		\end{column}
	\end{columns}
\end{standardframe}
