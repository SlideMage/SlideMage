		\shrinklists{1}
		\begin{itemize}
			\item \SMLineTop A top-aligned frame puts its first line on the Top Margin by itself
			\item \SMCmd{SMManualTopMargin} before a frame keeps your own position; \SMCmd{SMAutoTopMarginfalse} switches it off
			\item \SMCmd{SMIncreaseContentTopShift} and \SMCmd{SMReduceContentTopShift} have no effect any more
			\item Place your own overlays on these lengths; also \SMCmd{SMSideImageFrameWidth}, \SMCmd{SMImageBorderWidth}, \SMCmd{SMItemStep}
			\item \SMCode{check\_layout.py} measures a compiled PDF
		\end{itemize}
