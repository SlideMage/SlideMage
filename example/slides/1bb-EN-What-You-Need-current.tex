% SlideMage manual, chapter "Installing SlideMage": the overview of what to
% install. The next slides cover each program.
\setSlideVersion{1-1-0}
\begin{standardframe}{What you need}
	\small
	SlideMage is the program you work in. The PDFs themselves are typeset by LaTeX, which SlideMage calls for you, and an optional local AI model drafts image descriptions, translations and LaTeX source code. The first three programs are required; the following slides say where each one comes from and which
	choices matter during installation.
	\par\medskip
	{\footnotesize
	\begin{gothamtable}[gap=12pt]{lll}
		\toprule
		Program & What it is for & Where to get it \\
		\midrule
		MiKTeX or TeX Live & typesetting: LuaLaTeX, biber, latexmk & \SMCode{miktex.org}, \SMCode{tug.org/texlive} \\
		Perl (with MiKTeX only) & runs latexmk & Git for Windows, \SMCode{git-scm.com} \\
		SlideMage & the app & its releases on GitHub \\
		\midrule
		\emph{Optional:} Ollama and models & image descriptions, translation, AI & \SMCode{ollama.com} \\
		\emph{Optional:} viewer, pympress, \ldots & reading, presenting, editing & see \emph{Optional helpers} \\
		\emph{Optional:} Python 3.11+ & running SlideMage from source & \SMCode{python.org} \\
		\bottomrule
	\end{gothamtable}\par}
\end{standardframe}
