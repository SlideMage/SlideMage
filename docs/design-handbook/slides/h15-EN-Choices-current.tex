\begin{standardframe}{Do and do not}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Do:} use standard Card and RowButton. Exam Forge\textquotesingle s D10 comparison retained hierarchy and made selection clearer.
\item \textbf{Do not:} restore the sunken middle column or lifted white selection; the dark selected row was hard to find.
\item \textbf{Do:} keep controls at \SMCode{font\_control} and headings at \SMCode{font\_heading}.
\item \textbf{Do not:} merge every 14 px control into 15 px. D1\textquotesingle s renders enlarged inputs, checkboxes and lists.
\item Accept a merge only after checking both themes, long labels, narrow widths and keyboard focus.
\end{itemize}
\end{standardframe}
