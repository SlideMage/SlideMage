% SlideMage manual, chapter "Working in SlideMage": the checks before and
% after a compile (README: "Pre-render syntax check").
\setSlideVersion{1-0-0}
\begin{standardframe}{Safety nets}
	\small
	LaTeX's error messages are hard to read, and a compile takes time. So
	before every compile SlideMage checks the slides for the most common
	mistakes, and names the slide and the line.
	\par
	\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
		\begin{column}{0.47\linewidth}
			\scriptsize
			\begin{SMTable}[gap=8pt]{ll}
				\toprule
				Before every compile: & finds \\
				\midrule
				\SMCode{braces}, \SMCode{environments} & left open \\
				\SMCode{math-dollar}, \SMCode{math-delims} & math left open \\
				\SMCode{ampersand}, \SMCode{hash} & a stray \SMCode{\&} or \SMCode{\#} \\
				\SMCode{citation} & unknown key \\
				\SMCode{image}, \SMCode{code-file} & a missing file \\
				\SMCode{slide-file} & missing slide \\
				\SMCode{underscore}, \ldots & warnings \\
				\bottomrule
			\end{SMTable}
		\end{column}
		\begin{column}{0.5\linewidth}
			\shrinklists{2}
			\begin{itemize}
				\item An error stops the compile and asks: \emph{Cancel}, \emph{Show in editor} (at that line) or \emph{Render anyway}; warnings only go to the log. In the Source view the same check runs as you type, and \emph{Ask AI to fix} offers help
				\item It runs before every render: \emph{Render} in the Preview, \emph{Compile PDF}, \emph{Export PPTX}, \emph{Update slide previews}
				\item It reads the LaTeX as text, so it cannot find what only a real compile shows
				\item \SMCode{tools/check\_layout.py} in SlideMage's source repository is a separate command-line tool that measures a compiled PDF against the margins; \SMCode{\%\% layout-check: skip top} exempts a slide
			\end{itemize}
		\end{column}
	\end{columns}
\end{standardframe}
