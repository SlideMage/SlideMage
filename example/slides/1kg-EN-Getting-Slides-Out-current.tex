\begin{standardframe}{Getting slides out}
	\small
	Each compile turns a deck into PDFs: one per deck version you ticked. The
	options in the deck's row and under the deck table add other outputs.
	\par\medskip
	\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
		\begin{column}{0.47\linewidth}
			\shrinklists{1}
			\begin{itemize}
				\item The whole deck, or one PDF per chapter
				\item \emph{Presenter}: every slide next to its notes (\SMCmd{note}), for a presenter app such as pympress
				\item \emph{Export PPTX}: for PowerPoint's recording tools; reveal steps fade in one by one
				\item \emph{Generate appendix TOC}: an agenda and a grid of clickable previews at the end
				\item \SMCmd{deckoutputfolder}: where each PDF goes
			\end{itemize}
		\end{column}

	\end{columns}
